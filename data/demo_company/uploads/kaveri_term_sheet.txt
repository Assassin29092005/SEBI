--- BUSINESS ---

Business Description: Manufacture of precision-machined aluminium and steel components for automotive powertrain and braking assemblies, supplied to tier-one original equipment manufacturers
Industry Overview: Indian automotive component manufacturing, with demand linked to passenger-vehicle production volumes and a growing share of exports to South-East Asian assemblers
Products Services: Machined aluminium brake callipers, steel transmission shafts, cast-iron brake discs and precision sub-assemblies supplied to tier-one customers
Customer Concentration: Top three customers accounted for approximately 61 per cent of revenue from operations in the most recent financial year
Material Properties: Manufacturing facility at Plot 42, Peenya Industrial Area Phase II, Bengaluru, held on freehold; warehouse at Plot 17, Dabaspet Industrial Area, held on lease expiring 31 March 2031
Applicable Regulations: Factories Act 1948, Air and Water Pollution Control Acts with consent to operate from the Karnataka State Pollution Control Board, Legal Metrology Act 2009, and IATF 16949 quality system requirements applicable to automotive suppliers
Licences Approvals: Consent to operate from the Karnataka State Pollution Control Board valid to 31 March 2028, factory licence under the Factories Act 1948, GST registration and Importer Exporter Code
